# ZoneCheckr update, 12 Oct 2026

## Ads (Google Ad Manager)

**Why ads vanished and took minutes to come back**
- Every 60s the old code refreshed all slots at once. A refresh clears the ad on screen first, and when the new request came back unfilled, `collapseEmptyDivs(true)` collapsed the box. The next try waited for another 60s cycle, and one global "refresh in progress" flag could get stuck, which explains the 2 to 3 minute gaps.
- Slots were defined once for the whole visit. When someone moved to another page in the site's own navigation, the new page's ad divs could not request ads again, so they stayed blank until the timer happened to refresh them.
- The bottom anchor lives in the shared layout, so it never re-rendered between pages. That's why the same video ad kept playing.

**What changed**
- `components/AdSlot.tsx` now defines its slot when it appears and destroys it when it goes away. It is keyed on the URL path, so every page view gets fresh ad requests. That includes the bottom anchor, which now loads a new ad on each page.
- `app/layout.tsx` turns on GPT lazy loading (fetch 1.5 viewports ahead, render 0.5 ahead, doubled on mobile), so lower slots are ready by the time someone scrolls to them.
- Refreshes happen one slot at a time, and only after that slot has been at least 50% visible for 30 seconds while the tab is in the foreground. Slots that are off screen keep their current ad, so nothing goes blank while people scroll. If a refresh comes back unfilled, its space stays reserved and it tries again after 15 visible seconds.
- A placement that has never filled is hidden, so there's no empty "Advertisement" box.
- `lib/ads.ts` holds every placement in one place: ad unit path, div id and size mapping.

**New placements**
- Homepage: `homepage-health` sits just before "Start with a complete domain health check" (unit `zonecheckr_banner2`, div `div-gpt-ad-1790180731446-1`).
- Individual tool pages: `tool-end-content` sits after the explanatory article at the end of the page.
- Individual guide pages: `guide-end-content` sits after the last section, before the "Check your domain" call to action.
- Both of those use unit `zonecheckr_banner2` and div `div-gpt-ad-1790180731446-end-0`, capped at 728x90 or 336x280. The /tools and /guides index pages still have no inline ads.
- The new placements reuse your existing ad units. To report on them separately, create new ad units in GAM and change the `path` in `lib/ads.ts`.
- In GAM, mark these ad units as refreshing (Inventory > Ad units > Refresh) so AdX knows they refresh.

## Google Analytics (no traffic)
The site set every consent type, including `analytics_storage`, to **denied** for every visitor, and nothing on the page ever granted it. Your GTM screenshot shows exactly that: Denied as the default, with no update. GA4 therefore stored no sessions.

The site now uses region-specific Consent Mode defaults:
- Outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland, everything is granted, so GA4 counts visits normally.
- Inside the EEA, UK and Switzerland, everything stays denied until a consent banner grants it.

For those visitors, turn on a GDPR message in GAM under **Privacy & messaging**. It's Google's free certified consent tool, works with GPT, and sends the consent update automatically.

Also check in GTM that the GA4 tag isn't set to "Require additional consent". GA4 enhanced measurement should have "Page changes based on browser history events" switched on so page-to-page navigation is counted.

## Theme
Dark is now the default for new visitors. A visitor who already picked light keeps it.

## Mobile
- Footer: Tools and Resources sit side by side, Legal runs across a full row, links are larger touch targets, and the bottom line wraps cleanly.
- The body has bottom padding, so the fixed anchor ad no longer covers the footer.
- Fixed horizontal scrolling on the homepage at phone and tablet widths. The long "DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN" guide title was pushing the cards wider than the screen.

## Console messages
- The `[GPT] Render ended / Visibility / Impression viewable` lines were our own debug logging. They are gone. Add `?adsdebug=1` to any URL to see ad activity when testing.
- "PubAdsService.setTargeting is deprecated": fixed by moving to `googletag.setConfig({ targeting })`.
- "googletag.encryptedSignalProviders is deprecated": this isn't in the site code. It comes from a tag or partner script that adds secure signals, most likely a GTM tag or the ad partner's script. Ask them to switch to `secureSignalProviders`.
- "Permissions policy violation: unload is not allowed", "Slow network is detected… opensans" and "Powered by AMP": all three come from ad creatives running inside their own iframes. They're harmless, and the site can't change them.
